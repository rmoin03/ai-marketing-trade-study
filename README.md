# AI Marketing Trade Study

## Overview

This project evaluates leading generative AI platforms to determine their effectiveness for marketing-related tasks. The study compares multiple AI tools using a structured decision-making framework and assesses each platform's strengths, limitations, and overall suitability for business use.

## Objective

The objective of this trade study was to identify which AI solution provides the greatest value for marketing applications based on reliability, content quality, cost, and ease of use.

## AI Platforms Evaluated

* ChatGPT
* Gemini
* Claude
* Perplexity

## Evaluation Criteria

The platforms were assessed using the following criteria:

* Reliability and accuracy
* Content quality
* Cost and accessibility
* Ease of use
* Overall effectiveness for marketing workflows

## Methodology

A weighted decision matrix was developed to evaluate each platform against the selected criteria. Scores were assigned based on performance across multiple marketing-related tasks, and comparative analysis was conducted to determine overall rankings.

## Key Findings

* Identified strengths and weaknesses of each platform.
* Compared performance across common marketing use cases.
* Evaluated trade-offs between functionality, cost, and usability.
* Determined the most effective AI solution based on the selected evaluation criteria.
